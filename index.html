About Me

Hi, I'm Ryan Gomez, an artist and aspiring graphic designer. I was born and raised on O'ahu, HI, and I am currently pursuing a General Creative Media degree at the University of Hawai'i at West O'ahu. My love for art and design grew from countless childhood theme park visits and exploring pieces of vintage media that caught my attention. Those experiences remain a major source of inspiration in my work. I have so much to show you, and I can't wait to share it here with all of you!
